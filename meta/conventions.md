# MUS 602: conventions

The rules for both repos are in mus601's [meta/conventions.md](https://github.com/csuebmusic/mus601/blob/main/meta/conventions.md) (the two courses, who owns what, source of truth, git workflow, deployment, sources) and [meta/visual-conventions.md](https://github.com/csuebmusic/mus601/blob/main/meta/visual-conventions.md) (the house style). This file holds what is specific to mus602.

## style

Style, register, and feedback for everything in this repo follow the "how I want you to communicate" section of my Claude profile preferences.

## who owns what

- `README.md`: the face of the course, public-facing.
- `meta/`: planning and conventions, mine and Claude's. Markdown anywhere in the repo is mine and Claude's, except the READMEs. The repo is public, so all of it is readable at the repo and at the Pages URL.

## assessment

The iterative research paper (proposal, annotated bibliography, draft, revision, final), analytical assignments, seminar participation, and a conference-style presentation. Grades are A to F on the 0 to 4 grade-point scheme in mus601's [meta/assessment-scheme.md](https://github.com/csuebmusic/mus601/blob/main/meta/assessment-scheme.md). The 602 research expectations are in mus601's [meta/research-and-bibliography.md](https://github.com/csuebmusic/mus601/blob/main/meta/research-and-bibliography.md), under "into 602," until this repo has its own copy.
