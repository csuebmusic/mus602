# MUS 602: Analysis of Musical Styles
*Post-Tonality and Beyond*

A graduate research-and-analysis seminar at California State University, East Bay. 602 covers the turn of the century to World War II: Debussy through the Second Viennese School, Stravinsky, Bartók, and their contemporaries. The analytical vocabulary includes pitch-class set theory (set classes, interval vectors, Tn and TnI), symmetry and interval cycles, twelve-tone and serial technique, and atonal voice leading, held in a theoretical framework that is itself evolving and contested. As in 601, the real subject is becoming a scholar of this repertoire, forming research questions, evaluating sources, writing scholarly analysis with correct citation, and connecting analysis to historical and cultural context.

602 runs in the spring and takes up where 601, Birth and Death of Tonality, ends. It culminates in a research paper and a conference-style presentation.

## what's here

- `syllabus.md`: the full syllabus, with schedule, outcomes, assessment, and policies.
- `outline.md`: the schedule at a glance.
- `research/`: the research and citation apparatus, shared with 601 and adapted to the research paper.
- `units/`: week-by-week seminar material, added as the course builds.
- `tools/`: interactive analytical tools that compute and render in the browser.
- `assets/`: the repo's stylesheet, plus score excerpts, audio, and images.

Interactive tools are self-contained HTML and open in the browser through GitHub Pages, with no build step. This repo is being built out; materials land as the seminar develops.

## practical

Two units, on-ground or online-synchronous, graded A to F. The syllabus carries the current term's dates, meeting time, and logistics.
