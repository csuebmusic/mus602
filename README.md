# mus 602, analysis of musical styles

*Post-Tonality and Beyond*

MUS 602 is a graduate research-and-analysis seminar at California State University, East Bay. It covers the turn of the century to World War II: Debussy through the Second Viennese School, Stravinsky, Bartók, and their contemporaries. The analytical vocabulary includes pitch-class set theory (set classes, interval vectors, Tn and TnI), symmetry and interval cycles, twelve-tone and serial technique, and atonal voice leading, in a theoretical framework that's still contested. As in 601, the seminar's subject is becoming a scholar of this repertoire: forming research questions, evaluating sources, writing scholarly analysis with correct citation, and connecting analysis to historical and cultural context.

602 runs in the spring and takes up where 601, Birth and Death of Tonality, ends. It culminates in a research paper and a conference-style presentation.
